How to use the currency converter

Enter the amount you want to convert, choose the currency you have in the From box and the currency you want in the To box, then press Convert. Use the ⇄ button to swap the two currencies instantly.

Where do the exchange rates come from?

Rates are mid-market reference rates refreshed daily from a public exchange-rate feed. They're ideal for travel budgeting, online shopping, freelancing invoices and quick comparisons. Banks and money-transfer services usually add a margin, so the rate you actually receive may differ slightly.

Frequently asked questions

Is this currency converter free?

Yes. It's completely free, needs no sign-up and works on mobile and desktop.

Which currencies are supported?

More than 150 world currencies, including US Dollar (USD), Euro (EUR), British Pound (GBP), Indian Rupee (INR), Japanese Yen (JPY), UAE Dirham (AED), Canadian Dollar (CAD) and Australian Dollar (AUD).

How often are rates updated?

Rates update once a day. The time of the latest update is shown under the result.
